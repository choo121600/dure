# E3.1 — State survey (discovery engine)

The deterministic discovery engine of M3: `scripts/dure-survey.py` reads `.dure/` state and emits
forward-looking planning signals (report-only, JSON, exit 0/1/2). Ships with two pinned, falsifiable
signals — `closable-milestone` and `empty-epic` — each disjoint from `dure-doctor` (integrity),
`dure-audit` (debt), and `dure-status` (completion). Spec: [specs/dure-survey.md](../../specs/dure-survey.md).
