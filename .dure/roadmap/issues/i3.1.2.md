# I3.1.2 — closable-milestone signal

Surface a milestone that is ready to close: `status != done`, has at least one descendant, and every
descendant epic AND issue is `done`. The all-descendants oracle (not issues-only) is what keeps it
disjoint from `dure-status` completion% and prevents the false positive on `m2`. Report-only; `info`.
