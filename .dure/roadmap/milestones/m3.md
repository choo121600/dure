# M3 — Strategic planning

Self-discover "what is needed next" from the current state, then distill it through deep interview +
rigorous critique into milestones. dure already owns the convergence machinery (M1), so M3's new
capability is **proactive discovery**.

First feature: **dure-survey** — a deterministic, report-only survey of `.dure/` state that surfaces
forward-looking planning signals as falsifiable evidence ([specs/dure-survey.md](../../specs/dure-survey.md)).
A later epic (`/dure:direction`) turns those signals into a critiqued proposal behind a redteam sign-off gate.
